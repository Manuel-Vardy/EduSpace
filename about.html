ABOUT EDUSPACE

Experienced & Quality Solutions For 9+ Years

Bridging the gap between heritage and innovation.

We are a digital transformation consultancy and engineering company that delivers cutting edge solutions for global organizations and technology startups. Over the years we have been helping companies and established brands reimagine their business through digitalization.

EduSpace is a software development company in Ghana with over 9 years of market experience. Our development team drives excellence, ensuring we deliver exactly what your business needs. Trusted by giants public and private institutions in Ghana and Africa at large.

Our comprehensive approach includes consulting, engineering, and support services. From simple mobile apps to ERP cloud-based enterprise-level products. We reinforce your business with the combination of our expertise, resources, intelligence in technology, and passion for delivering innovative software solutions that make a difference.
